### MATCH CHECKLIST (General for all frames):

1.  **Subtle Background Grid:** A faint, dark grey grid across the entire display provides structure without distracting.
2.  **Consistent FUI Colour Palette:** Predominantly dark blue/teal background with bright cyan/green/blue as primary FUI accents, and red/yellow for warnings/highlights.
3.  **Layered & Overlapping Elements:** Components often have multiple concentric rings, overlapping panels, or elements that appear to float, adding depth.
4.  **Continuous, Subtle Animations:** Many small, independent animations (scrolling, rotating, pulsing, fluctuating) that run constantly, giving a sense of live data and system activity. Avoid jarring or overly fast movements.
5.  **Glow & Transparency Effects:** Liberal use of subtle glows on active lines, text, and indicators, combined with slight transparency on panels, to achieve the holographic, projected look.
